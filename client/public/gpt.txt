Q: How much does a golf cart tune-up cost?

Q: How often should I service my golf cart?
A: We recommend servicing your golf cart at least once a year or every 200-300 hours of use. Regular maintenance includes tune-ups, battery checks, brake inspections, and tire rotations.

Q: How long do golf cart batteries last?
A: Golf cart batteries typically last 4-6 years with proper maintenance. Lithium batteries can last 8-10 years. Signs of battery failure include reduced range, slow acceleration, and difficulty holding a charge.

Q: Can you service all golf cart brands?
A: Yes, we service all major golf cart brands including Club Car, EZ-GO, Yamaha, Star EV, ICON, Tomberlin, Cushman, and custom carts. We work on both electric and gas-powered golf carts.

Q: Do you install lift kits?

### Battery Questions

Q: How much does a golf cart battery replacement cost?
A: Single battery replacement costs $149-299, while a full battery pack replacement costs $599-1499 for lead-acid batteries. Lithium battery upgrades cost $1999-3999 but offer longer life and better performance.

Q: Should I upgrade to lithium batteries?
A: Lithium batteries offer significant advantages: 2-3x longer lifespan, 70% weight reduction, no maintenance, faster charging, and consistent power. They cost more upfront but often save money long-term.

Q: Why is my golf cart not holding a charge?
A: Common causes include old batteries, corroded terminals, faulty charger, or electrical issues. We recommend a battery load test ($39-59) and electrical diagnostic ($79-149) to identify the problem.

### Brake Questions

Q: How do I know if my golf cart brakes need service?
A: Warning signs include squeaking or grinding noises, reduced stopping power, the cart pulling to one side, or a spongy brake pedal. We recommend brake inspections at least annually.

Q: How much does brake service cost?
A: Brake inspection costs $49-79, brake pad replacement costs $89-149, and a complete brake overhaul costs $299-499. Regular brake maintenance is essential for safety.

### Motor Questions

Q: My golf cart is slow or won't move. What's wrong?

Q: How much does motor replacement cost?
